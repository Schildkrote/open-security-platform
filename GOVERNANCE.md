# Governance

open-security-platform is an open-source project governed by its maintainers and
community under the Apache-2.0 license.

## Principles

- **Openness:** development happens in public; decisions are documented.
- **Self-containment:** each component stays independently runnable and testable.
- **Safety first:** offensive and AI-security components enforce authorization,
  auditability, and non-destructive behavior by design.
- **Merit:** contributors earn trust through sustained, high-quality contributions.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** own the repository, review and merge changes, and set direction
  (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)).
- **Contributors** submit issues and pull requests.
- **Users** adopt components and provide feedback.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day changes are decided by lazy consensus among maintainers.
- Significant cross-component changes (layout, tooling, licensing, safety model)
  are discussed in issues/PRs before merging.
- Component owners have primary say over their component.

## Changes to governance

Changes to this document require maintainer consensus.
